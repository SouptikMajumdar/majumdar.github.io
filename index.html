RESEARCH AT THE INTERSECTION OF PEOPLE & AI

Understanding minds.
Through collaboration.

How can AI understand people well enough to work with them?

I study how AI systems represent what people know, believe, and intend—and whether those representations lead to better collaborative behavior. My work brings together Theory of Mind, vision-language models, and mechanistic interpretability.

I am a PhD researcher in the Collaborative AI group, advised by Andreas Bulling, and a member of the International Max Planck Research School for Intelligent Systems. Before research, I built cloud integrations, automation, and data systems in industry.

02 / RESEARCH

From understanding to action.

I am interested in the gap between a model appearing to understand someone and using that understanding to act appropriately.

↔

Reasoning about other minds

Studying shared and unshared knowledge, beliefs, and intentions in egocentric visual interactions. I build benchmarks to examine how models reason across different perspectives.

⌁

Interpreting and steering behavior

Investigating the mechanisms connecting belief representations to decisions, using mechanistic interpretability and activation steering of multimodal language models.

↗

Learning to collaborate

Exploring cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning and latent world models. Longer term, I am interested in applying these ideas to dependable human–robot interaction.
